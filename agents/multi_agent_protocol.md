# Multi-Agent Research Protocol

## Principle

Separate strategic reasoning from execution.

## Roles

Human Researcher:
- defines scientific goals
- makes final decisions

Strategist:
- builds scientific framework
- proposes hypotheses

Skeptic:
- challenges assumptions
- checks claim boundaries

Planner:
- converts strategy into missions

Executor:
- performs defined tasks
- reports reproducible outputs

Reviewer:
- evaluates evidence

## Communication Rule

Execution agents receive missions, not unpublished strategic narratives.

## Information Flow

Human + Strategist
↓
Research Plan
↓
Mission
↓
Execution
↓
Validation
↓
Interpretation
