# Contributing

## Principles

Contributions should improve scientific rigor, reproducibility, or workflow clarity.

## Guidelines

- Prefer general principles over domain-specific assumptions.
- Do not add unpublished research data or confidential examples.
- Preserve separation between strategy and execution.
- Every workflow change should include rationale.

## Review

New concepts should pass:

1. Scientific usefulness.
2. General applicability.
3. Compatibility with evidence-based reasoning.
