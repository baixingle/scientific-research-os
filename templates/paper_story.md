# Paper Story Template

## Purpose

Transform validated scientific evidence into a coherent research narrative.

## Structure

```
Existing framework
        ↓
Unresolved limitation
        ↓
New observation
        ↓
Physical insight
        ↓
Validation
        ↓
Broader implication
```

## Sections

## Existing Understanding

What does the field already explain?

## Limitation

What important question remains unresolved?

## Discovery

What new evidence was obtained?

## Interpretation

What does the evidence support?

## Boundary

What does the evidence not prove?

## Generalization

Why does this matter beyond the specific system?

## Principle

A strong paper explains a change in understanding, not only a collection of results.
