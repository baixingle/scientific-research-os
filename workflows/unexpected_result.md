# Unexpected Result Workflow

## Goal
Interpret surprising findings without immediately creating a mechanism.

## Steps

1. Record the observation without interpretation.
2. List alternative explanations.
3. Check data quality and reproducibility.
4. Identify the smallest additional analysis that separates hypotheses.
5. Update the evidence hierarchy.

## Required Questions

- What is directly observed?
- What is inferred?
- What remains unknown?
- What would falsify the current interpretation?
