# Literature to Strategy Workflow

## Purpose

Transform literature reading from information collection into research strategy development.

## Step 1: Establish Knowledge State

Identify:

- What is accepted?
- What is debated?
- What assumptions are commonly used?

## Step 2: Identify Limitation

Ask:

- What question remains unresolved?
- Why do existing methods fail?

## Step 3: Generate Opportunity

Construct:

Existing framework

↓

Limitation

↓

New opportunity

↓

Testable hypothesis

## Step 4: Design Minimal Test

Define:

- competing hypotheses;
- required evidence;
- decision after each possible outcome.

## Principle

Literature should guide questions, not replace independent scientific reasoning.
