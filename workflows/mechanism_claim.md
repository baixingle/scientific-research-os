# Mechanism Claim Workflow

## Purpose
Evaluate whether a proposed mechanism is supported.

## Checklist

1. Observation exists.
2. Temporal order is established.
3. A physical bridge is identified.
4. Alternative explanations are tested.
5. Perturbation or independent validation is available.

## Claim Levels

Observation -> Association -> Localization -> Mechanism -> General principle

Never promote a claim without evidence escalation.
