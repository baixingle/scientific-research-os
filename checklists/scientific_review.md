# Scientific Review Checklist

## Question

- Is the scientific question clearly defined?
- Is there a genuine knowledge gap?

## Evidence

- What is directly observed?
- What is inferred?
- What remains unknown?

## Mechanism

- Is there a physical bridge between observation and claim?
- Is temporal order established?
- Are alternative explanations considered?

## Validation

- Are results robust?
- Is independent validation available?
- Are conclusions transferable?

## Claim Boundary

- Does the claim exceed the evidence?
- What would a skeptical reviewer challenge?
