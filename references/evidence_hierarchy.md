# Evidence Hierarchy

Scientific claims must not exceed evidence.

## Level 1: Observation
Directly measured patterns.

Language: observed, associated, correlated.

## Level 2: Localization
Identify when, where, and how differences emerge.

Language: suggests, consistent with.

## Level 3: Mechanism
Requires physical linkage, temporal precedence, and validation.

Language: contributes, controls only when demonstrated.

## Level 4: General principle
Requires transferability and predictive validation.
